Geofabrik and Waymarked Trails derive from the same underlying OSM database and are not independent confirmations of a route. Source snapshots, raw response hashes, extraction bounds, filters and exceptions are recorded in the adjacent JSON reports.

Raw trail segments and OSM route relations do not establish access permission, current opening, legal difficulty, trail connectivity or field verification. The dataset includes no verified-route endorsements, invented ratings, guide profiles or booked departures.

Official website evidence consists of source URLs, checksums, dates and limited independently written factual summaries. The source articles and photographs are not redistributed. Each record specifies which facts were checked; it does not certify the OSM geometry.

No proprietary MAPS.ME, Wikiloc or AllTrails database or GPX library is included. No Waymarked Trails server implementation is copied into this project.
